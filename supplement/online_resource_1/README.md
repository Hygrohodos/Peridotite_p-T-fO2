## Provenance of the earlier level comparison (candour note, Section 3.4)
`data/reference/level_options_table1_v2_handrun.csv` is the hand-run comparison made during revision (v2 work,
11 Nov 2026). It has no generator. It scored several corrections, including a flat −96 °C level with no slope
(case D, then taken from the mean of the 52 xenoliths, validation included), on the validation set. That comparison
preceded the adoption of the median rule; the rule's value (−96.04 °C) coincides with case D. The v3 numbers at
−92, −96 and −102 °C are regenerated by `v3_level_sensitivity.py`.
